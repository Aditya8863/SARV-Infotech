A Journey Through Time

A glimpse into the life and work of Aryabhata.

476 CE — Birth

Aryabhata was born in the 5th century CE and later became one of India's greatest mathematicians and astronomers.

499 CE — Aryabhatiya

Around the age of 23, Aryabhata composed Aryabhatiya, a remarkable work covering mathematics and astronomy.

Mathematics & Astronomy

His work included calculations involving π, trigonometry, planetary movements and astronomical time calculations.
